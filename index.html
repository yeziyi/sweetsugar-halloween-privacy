Privacy Policy

Sweet Sugar: Halloween Match 3  |  Last updated: September 27, 2026  |  App version: 1.7.0

This Privacy Policy describes how Sweet Sugar: Halloween Match 3 ("the Game", "we", "our") handles information when you use our mobile application. By installing and playing the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The Game itself does not require account registration and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personally identifying information. Game progress (levels, coins, boosters, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

Advertising status of the current version: the version of the Game available today (and the build identified as version 1.7.0) does not contain any advertising SDK, does not display advertisements, and does not collect or share any data with advertising networks. The sections below about advertising apply to future versions in which advertisements may be introduced; they are published in advance so that you can see, before an update is released, what will change. Whenever advertising is actually enabled in an update, this page will state so explicitly and the "Last updated" date will change.

3. User-Generated Content and Uploads

Some features of the Game may allow you to create or upload content — for example selecting or taking a photo to use as a custom player avatar, saving a shared level or score image, or submitting content to a community or sharing feature ("User Content"). Where such a feature is offered:

  • Upload and storage: the file you choose is transmitted over HTTPS to our cloud storage provider and stored so that the feature can work. We do not read, scan or use your images for any purpose other than providing the feature you requested.
  • Generated URL: after upload, the service generates a unique web address (URL) that points to your uploaded file, so the Game (or the person you share it with) can display it. The URL is not derived from, and does not contain, your name, email address or other personal information.
  • Who can see it: URLs are effectively public — anyone who has the link may be able to view the content. Please do not upload content you consider private, or content that contains sensitive personal information, images of other people without their permission, or material you do not have the rights to.
  • Your responsibilities: you must own, or otherwise have permission to use and share, any content you upload. You must not upload unlawful, infringing, hateful, sexually explicit or otherwise harmful material.
  • Moderation and removal: we may remove any User Content that violates these terms or applicable law, and we may block uploads from accounts or devices used to post such content.
  • Retention and deletion: uploaded files are kept only as long as needed to provide the feature. You may request deletion of your uploaded content or of a generated URL at any time by emailing us (see Contact Us). Removing content inside the Game, or uninstalling the Game, does not by itself delete files already uploaded; please contact us if you want them removed from our storage.
  • Children: the upload features are not directed at children under 13. If we learn that content was uploaded by a child under 13, we will delete it.

If the version of the Game you are using does not include any upload or sharing feature, this section simply does not apply — no User Content is collected.

4. How Information Is Used

In the current version, which contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, no information leaves your device at all: gameplay data stays in local storage on your device. The uses listed below apply only if and when advertising or upload features are enabled in a future version.

  • Displaying and measuring advertisements, including rewarded video rewards
  • Ad personalization (where consent is given) or contextual/non-personalized ads
  • Fraud prevention and ad frequency capping
  • Aggregated analytics to improve gameplay experience

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Consent and Your Choices (GDPR / CCPA / COPPA)

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): In versions that include advertising, on first launch the Game presents a consent dialog. You may accept personalized ads or decline; declining results in non-personalized (contextual) ads only. You can change your choice at any time in the in-game Settings. The current version has no advertising and therefore shows no consent dialog.
  • US (CCPA/CPRA): In versions that include advertising, you may opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information via the in-game Settings or by resetting your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Children (COPPA): The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Where age is known to be under 13, ad networks are flagged to serve child-directed (non-behavioral) ads only.
  • Limit Ad Tracking: You can reset or disable your Advertising ID via Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" / iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. Data Retention

We do not store your data on our own servers. Local game progress remains on your device until you uninstall the Game. Third-party ad networks retain data according to their own privacy policies (see links above).

7. Data Security

All third-party SDK network traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

8. Permissions

The Game requests only the standard Android permission INTERNET. It does not request ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, the advertising-ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), or any other permission, because the current version contains no advertising SDK. If you use an upload feature that needs an image from your device, the Game may additionally request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+) or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (older Android versions), and/or CAMERA if you choose to take a photo directly. These permissions are used only when you actively use that feature, and you can deny them without affecting the rest of the Game. No access to contacts, microphone, or precise location is requested.
